Common Questions

Do I need to rebuild the rest of my website?

No. You can update additional pages whenever you want — or leave them as they are.

What website platforms can you work with?

We work with WordPress, Drupal, Webflow, and in some cases Wix. We can also build on a modern AI-managed web stack when that makes more sense.

Our goal is to work with what you already have whenever practical — not force you onto a new platform just to redesign your homepage.

Is this just a new template?

No. We custom-design the homepage around your company, brand, content, customers, and goals.

Will you update the content too?

Yes. We can improve the messaging, structure, calls-to-action, SEO, AIO, and search intent as part of the facelift.

What if my website really does need a complete rebuild?

We'll tell you. If the platform, structure, functionality, or overall site has bigger problems, a full redesign may be the better investment.
